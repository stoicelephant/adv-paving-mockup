## Problems with the current site (useful for the pitch)
1. **Every page is set to `noindex`**, which tells Google not to list the site. Confirmed in the page source of Home and Our Team. People searching for them can't find the site.
2. **Another company's photo** on the Services page (filename "Blalock-Paving…"). Using a competitor's work photo is a credibility and copyright risk.
3. **About page "Why Choose" heading links to prestigepavingconcrete.com**, a different company.
4. **Social icons go to Wix's own Facebook, Twitter and LinkedIn**, not AAC's (template leftovers).
5. **"Stripping" instead of "Striping"** on the home and services pages (stripping is removing paint; striping is painting lines).
6. **Footer says "© 2015 BizConnect Enterprises, LLC"**, which doesn't match the company name or the 2016 founding year.
7. Contact form only asks for project type: no name, phone or email fields appear.
8. Only one project on the Projects page, with a tiny collage image.
9. No hours, service area, or reviews anywhere.
10. Patricia's title differs between her heading ("Director of Operations and Marketing") and her bio ("Sales and Marketing Director").
